# 2-Nitrobenzaldehyde-pH-curves-DOPC
pH 7 and 9 with and without sea salt DOPC vesicle pH curve due to 2-nitrobenzaldehyde UV photolysis

DOPC vesicle samples were pH adjusted to 7 and 9 and again at 7 and 9 with the added condition of sea salt mixture. samples had 2-nitrobenzaldehyde added at time 0 so that the total concentration of 2-NBA was 1mM. Samples were run in triplicate and allowed to sit on a UV lamp at 365nm until graphs plateaued.
